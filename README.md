# Three foundational Quality Engineering concepts:

1. The Cost of Quality (CoQ) Pyramid: Investing early in Quality Assurance (Design & Incoming Material) reduces downstream Quality Control inspection costs and prevents catastrophic External Failure Costs (recalls, lawsuits).
2. Time-to-Market vs. Product Reliability: Rushing stages to cut schedule time increases defect leakages and damages long-term profit and brand equity.
3. Prevention (QA) vs. Inspection (QC): Quality cannot be "inspected" into a finished product; QA creates stable processes, while QC acts as a gatekeeper to filter out non-conforming items.
